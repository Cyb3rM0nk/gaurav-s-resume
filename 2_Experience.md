**What is VAPT?**

VAPT in security stands for Vulnerability Assessment and Penetration Testing a combined process used to identify, evaluate, and exploit security weaknesses in systems, networks, and applications. It includes automated vulnerability scanning and manual penetration testing to confirm which issues are truly exploitable.
